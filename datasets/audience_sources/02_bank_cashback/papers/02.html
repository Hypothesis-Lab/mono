Customer Segmentation Based on Client Behavior in Bank Cashback Loyalty Program

Student: Varvara Novozhilova

Supervisor: Pavel Sergeevich Molchanov

Faculty: St Petersburg School of Economics and Management

Educational Programme: Data Analytics for Business and Economics(Master)

Year of Graduation: 2025

This study aims to develop a behavior-driven customer segmentation model for cashback loyalty programs in banking, leveraging machine learning to optimize marketing strategies. Using debit card data from T-Bank, one of the major Russian banks, we analyzed spending patterns and cashback engagement across approximately 10,000 clients. We proved the positive influence of cashback on customers’ turnover and identified the partial relationship between cashback categories and elasticity of demand. Moreover, we have built two segmentations using dynamic K-Means clustering and Hidden Markov Models (HMM), which allowed us to find different interpretable customer segments, demonstrating distinct behavioral profiles. Furthermore, our study considered the transitions between customer segments over time, enabling us to capture the influence of cashback on the clients’ dynamic behavior. We also found that these segments differ in terms of sensitivity to cashback, meaning that one group reacts to some cashback category increasing the turnover in this category stronger than others. This research advances behavioral segmentation approach in fintech and provides banks with a scalable framework to enhance cashback loyalty programs through data-driven segments. Our findings add to an existing gap in the study of segmentation based on behavioral patterns typical of cashback loyalty programs in the banking sector.
