Segmenting customers into different groups using their characteristics and behaviors has always been an important topic. Customer segmentation can lead to better customer understanding and targeting, which in turn leads to more effective product tailoring and marketing strategies. This study uses anonymized UK Open Banking transactions from three months in 2017 and compares RFM-based K-Means and DBSCAN clustering. K-Means found three monthly clusters representing the most, medium, and least valuable customers and outperformed DBSCAN, whose silhouette coefficients were negative.
